Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Durva Fincorp collects through durvafincorp.cloud, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your consultation request and answer your questions
  • To arrange a meeting or call where you have asked for one
  • To keep records of client communication where required by regulation

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with third parties for marketing. We do not add addresses collected here to bulk marketing lists without your separate consent.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give by voluntarily submitting the form, and, where applicable, to comply with legal and regulatory obligations that apply to financial intermediaries in India.

5. Who processes it on our behalf

Form submissions reach us through a third-party form service acting as a Data Processor:

  • Web3Forms — receives your submission and forwards it to our inbox. See web3forms.com/privacy.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes standard server request data (such as IP address) to serve the page and protect against abuse. We do not have access to those server logs.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google servers when the page loads. No other third-party resources are loaded.

6. Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking technology. There is nothing for you to accept or decline.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become client relationships are deleted within 24 months. Where you become a client, records are retained for as long as the relationship continues, and thereafter for the period required by applicable financial regulation and tax law, whichever is longer.

8. Security

This site is served exclusively over HTTPS, so data you submit is encrypted in transit. Access to enquiry data is limited to Durva Fincorp personnel who need it in order to respond to you. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data of yours we hold
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data where we have no continuing legal reason to keep it
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as you gave it
  • Grievance redressal — complain to us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved
  • Nominate — appoint someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We aim to respond within 30 days.

10. Children

This site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us and we will delete it.
